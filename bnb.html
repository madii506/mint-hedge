01 · the vault

inside a Mint.

The vault is a Tokenbound account (ERC-6551): a smart account that belongs to the NFT. Whoever holds the NFT controls it, and nobody else can, including us.

vaultTokenbound account
goldXAUt · Tether Gold

the NFTgolddollarscrash token
  1. The NFT. An ERC-721 called Mint #N. Minting it also creates its vault, a Tokenbound account whose owner is always whoever holds the NFT.
  2. Gold. Part of your BNB buys XAUt (Tether Gold, one token per troy ounce) through KyberSwap, straight into the vault.
  3. Dollars. Part buys USDT into the same vault: the calm part that holds still.
  4. The crash token. Part buys SQQQon, Ondo's token for the ProShares UltraPro Short QQQ fund. It aims to move three times the opposite of the Nasdaq-100 each day, so it rises when tech stocks fall.
  5. Your call, any time. Empty the vault into your wallet in one transaction, or sell the NFT and the whole vault goes with it.

    The crash token aims for −3× the Nasdaq-100's return for one day. Gold is held flat here because it doesn't follow stocks.

    the gold

    XAUt tracks one troy ounce of gold. It moves with gold, not with stocks.

    the dollars

    USDT stays a dollar. It doesn't fall when stocks fall, and it doesn't rise either.

    the crash token

    SQQQon rises when the Nasdaq-100 falls. It resets every day, so over weeks of choppy markets it loses value even if the index ends flat. It's built for crashes, not for holding forever.
