how mint works.

Everything a mint does, written down: which accounts hold what, every instruction you sign, how the crash bet pays, what it costs, and what can go wrong. All numbers on this page are live.

overview.

A mint is a Metaplex Core NFT on Solana with a vault inside it. When you mint, part of your SOL is swapped into Gold xStock (GLDx), a token that tracks the SPDR Gold Shares ETF, and the rest into USDC. Both land in token accounts owned by the NFT itself. Optionally, $5.40 buys YES on a Polymarket question about the S&P 500 falling to a level you pick before December 31. That bet pays $1 per share if the index touches the level.

Burn the NFT at any time and the vault pays out to your wallet in the same transaction. Sell or send it and the vault goes with it. There's no mint fee and no admin key. This site builds the transactions; your wallet signs every one of them.

minting, step by step.

Your SOL is split first: $5.40 worth (plus 1.5% for swap slippage) goes to the bet if you chose one, 0.012 SOL is kept back for account rent and fees, and the rest is divided by your gold / dollars mix. Then your wallet signs these transactions in one approval, and they're sent in order:

  1. tx 1 · create

    Compute-budget price → Core CreateV2 (a fresh asset keypair co-signs, you pay, you own it) → create the gold vault and the dollar vault (associated token accounts, idempotent) → the 0-lamport index transfer.

  2. tx 2 · gold

    A Jupiter swap SOL → GLDx with destinationTokenAccount set to the gold vault, so the gold never touches your wallet. Slippage limit 1%.

  3. tx 3 · dollars

    A Jupiter swap SOL → USDC into the dollar vault. Slippage limit 1%.

  4. tx 4 · bet USDC

    Only with a bet: a Jupiter swap SOL → about $5.40 USDC into your own wallet.

  5. tx 5 · the order

    Only with a bet: Jupiter's prediction API builds a buy-YES order on your chosen market, deposit just under $5.40 USDC; you sign it as a second approval. Jupiter's keeper fills it and your position appears in a few seconds.

If tx 1 fails nothing else is sent. If a swap fails after tx 1, your mint exists with that part of the vault empty, and you can fill it from “my mints”. If the order fails, the USDC stays in your wallet.

redeem and top up.

Redeem is one transaction: create your own GLDx and USDC accounts if you don't have them, then for each vault a Core execute runs SPL transferChecked for the whole balance and closeAccount (rent back to you), signed by the vault signer inside Core, and finally Core BurnV1 burns the NFT. Only the current owner can do it. The bet is separate: claim it in “my mints” when the market resolves.

Top up: anyone can add gold or dollars to any mint's vault. It's a Jupiter swap with the vault as the destination. The vault only ever opens for the NFT's owner.

the crash bet.

The bet uses Polymarket's event “What will S&P 500 (SPX) hit by end of December?”, traded through Jupiter's prediction markets. Each “↓ level” market pays $1 per YES share if the S&P 500 trades at or below that level at any point before the market closes.

API.

One endpoint, /api/mint, running in Frankfurt. Everything is public. Transactions come back base64-encoded and unsigned (tx 1 is pre-signed by the new asset key only).

GET?a=status

prices (SOL, GLDx, SPYx, USDC with 24h change and liquidity), the crash event and its open ↓ levels with buy/sell YES prices, the mint count.

GET?a=quote&sol=0.25&gold=60&bet=1

the SOL split and live Jupiter quotes for the gold and dollar legs.

POST?a=build {wallet, sol, gold, bet}

returns asset, vault, n, split and txs[] (create, gold, dollars, bet-usdc), plus bet{market, usdc}. Add simulate: true to get tx 1's simulation.

POST?a=order {wallet, market, usdc}

a Jupiter prediction buy-YES order transaction for your wallet.

POST?a=send {tx}

relays a signed transaction and waits for confirmation.

POST?a=redeem {wallet, asset}

the execute + close + burn transaction; owner only. simulate: true supported.

POST?a=topup {wallet, asset, sol, what}

adds gold or dollars to any vault.

POST?a=claim {wallet, position}

claims a resolved bet.

GET?a=gallery · ?a=mine&wallet=

every mint from the index, its owner and vault balances; your mints plus your S&P positions.

GET?a=meta&id=&n= · ?a=img&id=&n=

the NFT's metadata JSON and its 512 px pixel image.

GET?a=selftest

simulates a full round trip on mainnet (below).

risks.

  • It's not a guaranteed hedge. Gold can fall with stocks. The bet only pays if the S&P 500 touches your level before the market closes; otherwise it's worth $0.
  • The issuer controls GLDx. Gold xStock is a tokenized security from Backed. Its mint has a permanent delegate and can be paused, which is normal for tokenized securities and means the issuer can move or freeze it. xStocks aren't offered to US persons.
  • Code risk. Your funds rely on Metaplex Core, the SPL token programs, Jupiter's swap and prediction programs, and Polymarket's resolution. A bug in any of them could cost money.
  • Thin markets. The crash levels trade lightly, and the buy/sell spread can be wide. Selling a bet early may return much less than you paid.
  • The bet isn't inside the NFT. It's in your wallet. If you sell the NFT, you keep the bet; if you lose your wallet, you lose both.
  • This site can go away; your vault can't. Everything is on-chain. Any tool that can call Core's execute as the owner can empty a vault without this site.
