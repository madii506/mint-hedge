  1. The NFT. A Metaplex Core asset in your wallet. Its address owns a vault that nobody else can open, us included.
  2. Gold. Part of your SOL buys Gold xStock (GLDx), a token that tracks a gold ETF, straight into that vault.
  3. Dollars. The rest buys USDC into the same vault. You set the split anywhere from all gold to all dollars.

  5. Redeem. Burn the NFT and the vault pays out to your wallet in the same transaction. Claim the bet when its market resolves.

    07 · fine print

    questions.

    Is this a guaranteed hedge?

    No. Gold doesn't always rise when stocks fall, and the bet only pays if the S&P 500 actually touches your level before December 31. Any part can lose value, and you can lose what you put in.

    Who holds the gold and the dollars?

    The NFT does. The vault's token accounts belong to the NFT's own signer address, a program-derived address of Metaplex Core. Only the NFT's owner can move them, through Core's execute instruction, and redeeming burns the NFT in the same transaction. This site never holds your funds.

    Where does the crash bet live?

    In your wallet, as a Jupiter prediction position on Polymarket's market. It is not inside the NFT, so selling the NFT doesn't sell the bet. Claim it in “my mints” after the market resolves.

    What is Gold xStock?

    GLDx is a Solana token from Backed's xStocks that tracks the SPDR Gold Shares ETF. Like every tokenized security, its issuer can pause or move it. xStocks aren't offered to US persons; check what applies where you live.

    Why can't I add a bet from the US?

    Jupiter's prediction markets are closed to the US and South Korea. You can still mint with gold and dollars only.

    What does it cost?

    There's no mint fee. You pay Solana fees, swap costs, and about 0.007 SOL of account rent, which comes back when you redeem.

    Can I sell or send my mint?

    Yes. It's a Metaplex Core NFT, and whoever owns it owns the vault.

    Are you part of Polymarket, Jupiter, Backed or Metaplex?

    No. mint is independent and uses their public tools. Nothing here is financial advice.
