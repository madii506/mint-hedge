01 · the vault

inside a mint.

vault ownerthe NFT itself
goldGLDx · Token-2022

to cash out1 transaction
the NFTgolddollarscrash bet
  1. The NFT. A Metaplex Core asset in your wallet, named mint #N. Its address has a signer of its own, a program address with no private key, and that signer owns the vault. Nobody else can open it, us included.
  2. Gold. Part of your SOL buys Gold xStock (GLDx), a token that tracks the SPDR Gold Shares ETF. Jupiter routes the swap straight into the vault, so the gold never sits in your wallet.
  3. Dollars. The rest buys USDC into the same vault. You set the split from all gold to all dollars: gold for the long haul, dollars for something that holds still.
  4. The crash bet. $5.40 buys YES on a live Polymarket question: will the S&P 500 dip to your level by December 31? Pick a shallow level for better odds or a deep one for a bigger payout. It pays $1 a share if it hits.
  5. Redeem. Burn the NFT and the vault pays out to your wallet in the same transaction, account rent included. Claim the bet when its market resolves. Or sell the NFT, and the vault goes with it.

    Live from Polymarket's “What will S&P 500 (SPX) hit by end of December?” via Jupiter. A bet costs the YES price per $1 it pays.

    the gold

    Moves with the price of gold, not with stocks. In some sell-offs gold held up and in some it didn't. That's why it's a mix and not a promise.

    the dollars

    Stay a dollar. They don't fall when stocks fall, and they don't rise either. They're the calm part of the vault.

    the bet

    Pays $1 a share if the S&P 500 touches your level before December 31, and goes to zero if it doesn't. The deeper the level, the cheaper the share and the bigger the payout.
